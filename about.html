About Mr. Burn

The person behind the experience at YL Recreation Park

📸 About the Staff

Mr. Burn is a dedicated staff member at YL Recreation Park, Kaki Bukit, Perlis.

👤 Personal Background

Mr. Burn is 29 years old and has been working at YL Recreation Park since its opening in December 2024. He has been involved in the daily operations of the park from the very beginning.

He is known among visitors for his friendly personality and willingness to help. Despite not having many years of experience, he has quickly adapted to his role and gained valuable skills through real-life interactions with visitors.

🌿 Natural Environment

YL Recreation Park is surrounded by a beautiful natural environment, including various types of trees such as the famous Harumanis mango tree. Visitors are allowed to pick the fruits when they are ripe, making the experience more enjoyable.

The park is also home to around 8 to 9 species of birds. For example, owls can be seen at night, while herons and kingfishers come during the day to feed on fish in the pond. This creates a peaceful and unique atmosphere.

✨ Experience & Story

One of the most interesting experiences for Mr. Burn is meeting visitors from different backgrounds, including students from UniMAP. He often shares information about the park and its attractions.

However, one of the challenges he faces is explaining the park to visitors who are not familiar with the place. This requires patience, communication skills, and confidence.

🎯 Purpose of the Park

YL Recreation Park is designed as a recreational and healing space for people of all ages. It is suitable for children, teenagers, and adults to relax and enjoy nature.

Overall, the park provides a meaningful experience by combining nature, relaxation, and simple recreational activities in one place.
